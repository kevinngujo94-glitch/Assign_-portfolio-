ABOUT ME

I'm interested in understanding how things connect.

My interest in networking started with a simple question: how do all these devices actually communicate with each other?

The more I learn about networking, the more interesting it becomes. IP addresses, routing, switching, protocols and troubleshooting are all pieces of a much bigger system.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, but I'm taking the process seriously. I want to understand the fundamentals properly, gain practical experience and eventually work with real network infrastructure.
